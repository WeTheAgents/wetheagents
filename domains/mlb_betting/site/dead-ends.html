Dead Ends

Strategies tested and rejected — and why
11 approaches tested and killed. Each dead end represents hours of research, feature engineering, and backtesting. They are documented here so we never revisit them without new evidence.
Favorite ML Flat
DEAD Sessions: 4, 6, 9 • ML
Vig eats 4-5%. WR 56-60% required, achieved ~54-56%. Consistently -3% to -5% ROI across all filter combinations.
Favorite RL -1.5 (unfiltered)
DEAD Sessions: 4 • RL -1.5
Baseline 42.3% cover, needs 50% at 2.3 odds. Gap is 8 percentage points — no filter closes it without obliterating volume.
Favorite Series Dogon
DEAD Sessions: 9 • ML series
Gap -2.2pp vs breakeven 82.7%. Favorites don't win BOTH games in a series reliably enough. Achievable max: 80.5%.
Series Dogon + Pitcher Features
DEAD Sessions: 2, 3 • ML series
Martingale risk profile with +3-5% ROI — worst of both worlds. When it loses, it loses big (double-stake on G2 gone). Superseded by strategies with cleaner mechanistic edges and 3-6x higher ROI. Not worth the variance.
Home Underdog +1.5 RL
DEAD Sessions: 11 • RL +1.5
Odds too low (1.61-1.72). Breakeven 58-62% but cover peaks at 54.6%. No filter combination profitable. Home dogs just lose by too much.
Home Underdog ML
DEAD Sessions: 11 • ML
WR peaks at 50.3%, breakeven ~50.5%. No margin at any threshold.
F5 (First 5 Innings) ML
DEAD Sessions: 4 • F5 ML
Push rate 14.8% kills edge. -19.8% to -23.1% ROI. F5 market is structurally unprofitable for ML bets.
OVER Totals (ML Model)
DEAD Sessions: 20 • O/U Over
AUC 0.50 — no signal. Tested: UNDER inversion, dedicated model (22 features), minimal model (11 features). Offensive explosions are inherently unpredictable. Market prices recreational OVER bias.
NRFI ML Classifier
DEAD Sessions: 21, 22 • NRFI
Base rate 49.2% (myth was 57%). 18 PA per half-inning = structural noise ceiling. AUC 0.52 is the limit. Best ROI: +5.6% on 122 bets (3/5 seasons). Not reliable.
Coinflip Zone (Momentum + Structure Duel)
DEAD Sessions: 13, 14, 23, 24 • ML (even)
40.5% accuracy, -22.1% ROI. Experts cancel each other: 80% away bias, confidence miscalibration (0.65-0.75 conf = 20% accuracy). Zero expert diversity.
Savant Bullpen Statcast
DEAD Sessions: 28 • ML
Market already prices Statcast data (public since 2015). xwOBA mismatch: 53% WR at -0.2% ROI. Fatigue signals are ANTI-predictive (-5% to -13%). CatBoost importance: 1.74%.
Away RL +1.5 (real odds)
DEAD Sessions: 4, 9 • RL +1.5
Session 4 discovered real odds are 1.57 (not 1.87 assumed). At 1.57, breakeven is 63.7% but baseline cover is 60.2%. Gap too wide. Only works with specific filters (bullpen day, fatigue).
Why These Failed — Common Patterns
Vig Trap
Favorite ML, Home Dog, F5 ML. Market prices are close to fair; the 4-5% vig makes breakeven impossible. Need either extreme mispricing or a structural edge to overcome.
Signal Ceiling
NRFI (18 PA = noise), CF zone (experts cancel), OVER (entropy). Some markets have fundamental noise floors that no model can break through.
Market Efficiency
Savant Statcast (public since 2015), Favorite ML (sharp money). When data is public and widely used, the market already prices it in.
