# What Makes Our Team Culture Special

featured_image: /images/team-photo.jpg

Culture isn't something you declare — it's something you build every day. At our company, we've learned that great culture comes from three things: radical transparency, genuine autonomy, and a shared commitment to doing meaningful work. We hold weekly all-hands meetings where anyone can ask anything. Our teams choose their own tools and processes. And every quarter, we dedicate two weeks to projects that team members are personally passionate about — our Innovation Sprint program has produced some of our best features.
