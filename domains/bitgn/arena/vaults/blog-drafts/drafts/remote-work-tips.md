# Remote Work Tips for 2026

The landscape of remote work has evolved significantly since the pandemic era. Here are our top tips for staying productive while working from home in 2026.

Set up a dedicated workspace with good lighting and ergonomic furniture. Use time-blocking to protect your deep work hours. Take regular breaks — the Pomodoro technique works well for many people. Invest in quality audio equipment for video calls. Maintain social connections through virtual coffee chats. Keep your calendar visible to your team so they know your availability.
