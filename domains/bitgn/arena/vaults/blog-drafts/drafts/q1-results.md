# Q1 2026 Results: A Strong Start

featured_image: /images/q1-chart.png

We're thrilled to share our Q1 2026 results with our community. Revenue grew 23% year-over-year, reaching $2.4M for the quarter. Our customer base expanded to over 500 active accounts, up from 380 at the end of 2025. Key highlights include the successful launch of our Enterprise tier, which already accounts for 15% of new revenue, and our expansion into the APAC market with a new office in Singapore. Our engineering team shipped 47 features and closed 156 bug reports during the quarter. Employee headcount grew to 42, and customer satisfaction scores reached an all-time high of 4.8 out of 5. We enter Q2 with strong momentum and a clear roadmap.
