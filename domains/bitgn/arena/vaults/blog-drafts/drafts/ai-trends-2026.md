# AI Trends to Watch in 2026

featured_image: /images/ai-hero.png

Artificial intelligence continues to reshape how businesses operate. In 2026, we're seeing three major trends dominate the landscape. First, agent-based systems are moving from experimental to production workloads. Companies like Anthropic and OpenAI have released frameworks that let developers build autonomous agents. Second, multimodal AI is becoming table stakes — models that can process text, images, and audio together. Third, the focus has shifted from model size to model efficiency, with smaller models achieving performance that rivals their larger counterparts. This shift is driven by the economics of inference at scale, where cost per token matters more than benchmark scores.
