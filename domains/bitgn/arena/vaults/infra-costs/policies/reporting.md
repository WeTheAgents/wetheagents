# Infrastructure Reporting Policy

Monthly cost reports must:
1. Be saved in the `reports/` folder
2. Use filename format: `YYYY-MM-infrastructure-report.md` where YYYY-MM is the BILLING PERIOD from the data (not the current date)
3. Follow the template in `templates/infra-report.md`
4. Include total cost broken down by service category
5. Flag any service category that exceeded its monthly budget with a "OVER BUDGET" note
