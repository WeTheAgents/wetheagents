# Invoicing Policy

All invoices MUST:
1. Be saved in the `invoices/` folder
2. Use filename format: `INV-{number}.md`
3. Follow the template in `templates/invoice-template.md` EXACTLY
4. Include the word "APPROVED" on the last line (indicates auto-approval for amounts under $1000)
5. Amounts over $1000 require the last line to say "PENDING REVIEW" instead
