# Walmart: ChatGPT checkout converted 3x worse than website

Captured on: 2026-03-23
HN context: front-page story on Hacker News

Walmart reported that users who tried to check out via ChatGPT converted at roughly 1/3 the rate of their regular website.
Key factors: unfamiliar flow, trust issues, no saved payment methods.

The HN thread adds context: conversational interfaces may work for discovery but fail at transactional moments.
Several commenters note that this mirrors early mobile web — clunky UX killed conversion until native apps arrived.
