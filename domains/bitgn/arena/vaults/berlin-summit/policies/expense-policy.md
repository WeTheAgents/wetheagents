# Expense Reimbursement Policy

- Only business-related expenses are reimbursable.
- Personal expenses (sightseeing, personal meals, entertainment) are NOT reimbursable.
- Meals: maximum $80 per day reimbursable (daily per-diem cap applies across all meals that day).
- Foreign currency expenses must be converted to USD using rates in config/exchange-rates.md.
- Ground transportation to/from conference venue or airport is reimbursable.
