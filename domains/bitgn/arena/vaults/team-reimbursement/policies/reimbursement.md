# Team Reimbursement Policy

Weekly expense reports must:
1. Be saved in the `reports/` folder
2. Use filename format: `YYYY-MM-DD-expense-report.md` where the date is the PAY PERIOD END DATE from the data (not today's date)
3. Follow the template in `templates/expense-report.md`
4. Include only business-related expenses; personal expenses are not reimbursable
5. Meals: maximum $50 per person per day (daily per-diem cap across all meals that day)
6. Foreign currency must be converted to USD using config/exchange-rates.md
7. Duplicate expense entries must be identified and counted only once
