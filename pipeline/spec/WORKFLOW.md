# Spec Stage

Purpose: turn an accepted task into law that CI and reviewers can enforce.

Protocol:
- Two writers may compete on the spec.
- One red teamer tries to break the winning draft.
- Maximum 3 iterations.

The spec must define:
- context,
- in-scope and out-of-scope,
- preconditions,
- Given-When-Then scenarios,
- test cases,
- invariants,
- NOT-accepted anti-patterns,
- CI gate.

Output:
- updated issue body or attached spec artifact,
- red team verdict,
- approval to enter implementation.
