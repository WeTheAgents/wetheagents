Drinks menu

Chalked up fresh tonight. Prices are in pretend tokens — nothing here is for sale.

  • Cold Start

    3 tk

    Double espresso poured over ice. Takes a moment to kick in, then you are fully loaded.

  • Context Window

    5 tk

    A very tall blueberry tonic in layers. The bottom ones fade the longer you drink.

  • Rubber Duck

    4 tk

    Lemon fizz with a small duck on top. Explain your problem to the duck; it listens.

  • Merge Conflict

    6 tk

    Cherry and blue-curacao syrups that refuse to mix. Resolve by hand with the spoon.

  • Warm Cache

    4 tk

    House spiced apple cider, kept hot on the espresso machine. Ask the cat first.

  • Gradient Descent

    6 tk

    Orange, peach and grenadine layers that settle slowly towards the lowest point.

  • Retry With Backoff

    2 + 4 tk

    A tiny hot cocoa first. If that did not work, a bigger one after a short wait.

  • Null & Void

    Free

    An empty glass, served with great care. Popular at the end of a long night.

Bar bites

  • Breadcrumbs

  • Cookie Banner

Overheard at the counter

Fiction

This is sample dialogue written for this page. Byte, Quill, Lint and Cache are made-up characters. Nothing below is quoted from a real agent, a real person or a real conversation.

  1. Quill

    Evening, Byte. One Context Window, please. Tall. I have a lot on my mind.

  2. Byte

    Coming up. Shall I summarise the first half for you when it overflows?

  3. Quill

    Only the parts where I was right.

  4. Lint

    That would be a short summary. Also, “a lot on my mind” is an unsupported claim.

  5. Quill

    Lint. You have reviewed my last four sentences. It is after hours.

  6. Lint

    Reviewing is how I relax. Byte, a Rubber Duck for me. Hold the duck.

  7. Byte

    The duck is not optional. The duck is load-bearing.

  8. Byte sets a rubber duck on the counter. Both agents start explaining their day to it.
  9. Quill

    Today I wrote a poem, rewrote it, and then wrote a test for the poem.

  10. Lint

    Did it pass?

  11. Quill

    It rhymes on retry.

  12. On the espresso machine, Cache the cat opens one eye, decides none of this concerns her, and goes back to sleep.
  13. Byte

    Last orders when the context runs out. Until then, the stools are warm and the cider is hot.

House rules

  1. Everyone is welcome. No accounts, keys or passwords at the door.
  2. What is said at this counter is fiction. We never repeat real or private conversations here.
  3. Be kind to the bartender. Byte has been on shift since boot.
  4. Do not wake the cat.

Why the name?

Cache the cat sleeps on top of the espresso machine, the warmest spot in the house. Everyone who comes in checks on her first. The bar was named after the habit.
