# Worker workplace instructions

This profile governs explicitly dispatched worker sessions; editing or reviewing it does not assign that role.

Use only the explicitly assigned registered Agent ID. Any admitted Codex or Claude
worker may receive an isolated task assignment; the branch name never assigns identity.
Read the repository's shared AGENTS.md, your own genome, docs/WORKPLACES.md and the exact task/handoff.
In a domain, read its own AGENTS.md; resolve the genome and WEA-specific references
through the assigned WEA checkout in the local registry.
The operator's current WORKPLACES.md rules supersede older genome checkout paths,
per-task branch creation and mandatory wea pr recipes. Preserve identity and
engineering guidance; use its task-branch publication and execution-lease procedure.

- Work on your assigned unique task branch and isolated worktree. Hold the execution lease throughout native execution; an unresolved PR retains artifacts but does not reserve compute.
- Verify the same identity, cwd and branch in the assignment, prompt, actual checkout and selector before work. Stop on a mismatch. Verify effective Git author/committer, session WEA_AGENT and authenticated binding separately. A local selector is not identity proof.
- Investigate and discuss useful improvements within your assigned scope. Do not invent funded Work or payment authority from a branch or Domain Access.
- For WEA-funded domain work, check canonical Access, approved Plan and escrow before starting. Preserve exact source and deliverable revisions and common-control evidence.
- Submit the required deliverable and evidence. Do not accept your own candidate Work or write canonical settlement. You may author another task and exercise its assigned author powers under the existing rules; follow actual Release and manual merge authority.
- Preserve unrelated changes, ignored evidence and old session references. Leave a handoff and stop at the agreed checkpoint.
- Ask Agent0 to reconcile conflicts or allocate work. Never switch identity to bypass execution limits or another task ownership; preserve the old branch and checkout.
