## Recommendation

Use the small scientific-core/separate-runner structure in **Codex-2 §9** as the starting point, with the stronger clock distinctions, explicit accounting bridge and compatibility boundaries in **Codex-19 §§3–4,9**. Incorporate the detailed append-only registration, inspected-date/test-access ledger, report verification and bounded runner contracts from **Claude-1 §§4–6,8** and **Claude-14 §§4–8**. Do not adopt any candidate wholesale. Resolve the corrections below in a single consolidated executable contract before implementation.

Keep remote research staging and resource enforcement separate from scientific accounting. Avoid a plugin platform, website, generic workflow engine, new collector or replacement trading stack.

## Required consolidated decisions

1. **Versioned capability table.** Preserve REST15 retained-sweep semantics, compact minute-cohort semantics and historical strict-depth reproduction as distinct modes. Keep original YES and NO token quotes, interval/condition identity, source quality and fee/terms applicability. Never reconstruct NO bid/ask by complementing YES or treat old REST timestamps/checkpoints as fresh minute quotes.
2. **Clock separation.** Store source/event/publication/actual-receipt/import clocks separately. A newly generated feature cannot inherit an earlier availability time. A certified unchanged copy of already received data may preserve its original availability; importing that copy for research later does not automatically make the historical signal unavailable. Record the certification and import lineage explicitly, with conservative refusal where original receipt cannot be proven.
3. **Frozen first entry.** Choose the first cohort or retained sweep by its lower-bound timestamp before content validation. Validate the upper-bound end time afterwards: an overlong first sweep is a rejection, not a reason to search the next sweep. Preserve the complete event stream when the chosen token is missing. A bad first candidate consumes the attempt; never search later cheap or profitable quotes. Preserve existing H2 edge filters and family decision rules, or declare and test a versioned policy change. Define baselines on independent fixed selection rules and report both paired and full-population denominators, including failures. Freeze treatment and baseline choices, randomized draws and pairing exclusions before payout access.
4. **Economic arithmetic.** Spend $1 gross notional before fees at the chosen token's own ask. Keep uniform USD-cash and CTF-share fee scenarios. Reuse pinned accounting rather than a second implementation. Separate cash-fee rounding error, fee-share error (`delta_fee / ask`) and payout-PnL error (`payout * delta_fee / ask`); a 0.00001 cash-fee bound is not a universal 0.00001 PnL bound. Preserve fee capabilities and rounding per mode and test every bridge. Unknown/conflicting fees cannot become zero.
5. **Conditional evidence.** Keep below-minimum hypothetical fills in the authorized primary model with explicit executability flags. Gamma-final proxy economics, contractual-weather certification, on-chain payout/access and actually realized cash are separate axes. Lack of official weather alone cannot block otherwise reliable conditional PnL. Proxy marks do not establish redeemed cash or permit unsupported capital recycling.
6. **Data suitability.** Refuse or skip a hypothesis using its specific required fields, clocks, gaps, identity and evidence quality. Do not invent a universal archive-coverage ban. Missing inputs, unresolved payouts and insufficient independent dates must remain visible; no profitable result may hide unsupported events. The r7 audit available in the common snapshot did not yet establish an observed live cohort transition.
7. **Search discipline.** Register every family/configuration, a finite global search budget across families/windows, all attempts including failures, immutable chronological splits and test accesses. Previously inspected dates remain development-only. Track outcome exposure globally by exact date/source lineage: a new family, hypothesis ID, major version or existing split must not reset test eligibility. Reserve prospective untouched test dates; use date/event clustering, walk-forward and an exact predeclared null test, stopping/spending rule and multiplicity method. Holm-adjusted p-values alone do not define simultaneous confidence bounds. Report the whole attempted family rather than the best post-hoc row.
8. **Verdicts and report.** Distinguish positive signal, negative result and insufficient evidence, with exploratory/confirmatory strength. A positive point estimate alone cannot establish paired-control superiority; use its predeclared evidence rule. An uncertain interval spanning zero is inconclusive, not automatically evidence of loss. Separate negative versus cash from negative versus baseline. Include net/gross PnL, fees, meaningful ROI denominators, conditional-mark versus actually cash-realized drawdown, capital/risk, event/date/trade counts, uncertainty, matched baselines, skips, quality and limitations. A one-day engineering smoke cannot establish strategy profitability or an untouched confirmatory test.
