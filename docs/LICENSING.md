# Licensing and provenance

The operator chose MIT on 2026-10-02 for project-owned code and documentation.
The [LICENSE](../LICENSE) records that choice. It does not establish ownership of every included material.

The MIT grant applies to material whose copyright holders authorize that distribution.
Third-party code, documentation, data, models, and results retain their own terms and attribution requirements.
External dependencies are not relicensed by this repository. A source citation is not redistribution permission.

A fresh source snapshot does not change earlier distributions or their license declarations.
Maintainers must establish permission before offering previously restricted material under MIT;
preserve applicable original notices and do not infer ownership solely from Git author names.

Repository visibility and licensing are separate decisions. Public readiness retains the unresolved
historical and third-party provenance questions; a notice scan is not a complete rights clearance.

Historical AGPL-family license declarations remain in Git history. The current MIT choice does not rewrite those declarations or establish permission to relicense third-party or previously restricted material.
